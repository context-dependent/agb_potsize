Measuring…
    Booth setup and marker
    1. The marker card stands upright, with its centre on the same line as the centre of the pot spot, and faces the camera squarely. The app measures the pot in the plane of the marker, so the two must be the same distance from the camera.
    2. Stand several pieces side by side along the marker line to price them from one photo. Leave about 2 cm between pieces, or two touching pieces are measured as one, and 5 cm between the last piece and the card.
    3. Use a matte, evenly lit backdrop in a colour few glazes share. Chroma-key blue works for most work. Keep a grey sheet for blue-glazed pieces.
    4. Diffuse light from both sides keeps the floor shadow faint, so it isn't counted as part of the pot.
    5. Print the marker at 100% scale and check its black square with a ruler. Enter that measurement in the settings below.
    Studio settings

    Raise it if the backdrop or shadow is being counted as pot. Lower it if pale parts of the pot are cut off.
